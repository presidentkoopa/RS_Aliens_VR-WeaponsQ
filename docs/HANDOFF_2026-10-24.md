**There is now a behaviour harness, and it works.** `tools/rtest/` runs the pack in the real

compiles". It caught a VM abort on its first outing.

```bash
cd tools/rtest
python -B suite.py                    # all six magazine guns, pass/fail per gun
python -B run.py NAME 110 "10:god; show_messages 0; aet_gun Loader_3D" "45:aet_loader 1" "260:echo END"
```

`tools/check.sh` is the fast one: packs the set, loads it **with the TC**, prints
`*** DID NOT COMPILE ***` or a clean line. Run it after every edit. It cannot see a VM abort —
that is what the harness is for.

**Both of them found more faults in my own test code than in the pack.** Read §5 before
trusting any number in a report.

## 3. IN THE WORKING TREE, HALF FINISHED

**This is the part to pick up.** The owner asked for the melee reference's "spine" and the
remaining fixes; the spine is in and compiling, the rest is partly done.

### Done and compiling

- **`zscript/rsvr/pose.zs` — the spine.** A per-player, per-hand ring of 8 samples of
  `{hand point in the PAWN'S FRAME, pawn pos, tic stamp}`, filled once a tic by
  `RSVR_PoseKeeper` (registered first in `mapinfo.txt`). `Speed()`, `Travel()` and `Delta()`
  read it. Storing the hand in the pawn's frame subtracts a turn **exactly, at any turn rate**,
  which is what replaces `RSVR_HandMotion.Bogus()`'s 10°-per-tic flush. The pawn's position
  beside it makes a teleport visible, so that sample is refused on evidence.
  **Not networked** — the owner said skip multiplayer. The shape is the networked one: a
  netevent would feed `Push()` and nothing downstream changes.
- **`AE_LoaderNoise`** and `Say(mo, at, snd)` in `loader.zs`: a one-tic carrier so a sound comes
  from the claw instead of from inside the player's chest. Five of the loader's sounds moved.
- **Reachability on the ram and the arc**, matching the swing's. The arc checks from the
  previous link, so the chain needs line of sight along its whole path.

### NOT done — pick up here

1. **Nothing reads the spine yet.** `pose.zs` is built, registered and compiling, but
   `loader.zs` still uses `RSVR_HandMotion` and `melee.zs` still differences raw hand points.
   Moving them over is the whole point of it and is the next job:
   - `AE_LoaderHandler.Claw()` — replace `armSpeed`/`spRing`/`handPrev` and the `Bogus()` call
     with `RSVR_Pose.Get().Speed(mo, h, 3)` and `.Travel(mo, h, 3)`.
   - `RSQ_MeleeHandler` — same for `dh`/`dq` and the `travelled[]` window.
   - When they are moved, `RSVR_HandMotion.Bogus()` and the `yawDelta` machinery in
     `loader.zs` become dead and should go, or the next reader will think they still matter.
2. **The crush has no reachability test** (`loader.zs`, the `ae_crush` branch). The ram and arc
   now have one; the crush does not, though it matters least — you must already be holding the
   thing.
3. **The free fist is still speed-only** (`melee.zs`, `so >= need`). The reference's BD22 punch
   has four conditions: fast, mostly forward **in the body frame**, a real jab by displacement
   (`JAB_LEN` forward inside the window), and the hand ending `FRONT` ahead of the body. The
   spine gives all four cheaply — that is the first thing it should be used for.
4. **Two loader sounds still play on the player**: the hum and the footstep/clunk pair. The hum
   is a loop and wants the document's "carried emitter … volume re-asserted every tic with 0
   handled explicitly", not a one-tic carrier.
5. **The satchel grip looks loose** — its cross-section
   (`docs/renders/fit/xsec_ship_Satcheler_3D.png`) shows the plate passing near the fingers
   rather than clasped. It is a flat charge, not a handle, so "enclosure" is the wrong measure
   and it needs a judgement, not a score.

## 4. Left undone, deliberately

- **Nothing is baked into `handtable.zs` except the smartgun's off hand.** Placement is
  `hook + slider` **added** (`hands.zs:150`) — "the owner tunes in the headset and bakes the
  numbers into GunHook afterwards". So baking is one step with two halves. `ae_hands_reset`
  (KEYCONF) zeroes all 24 sliders in one command, which is the second half. Replacing the
  owner's own tuning with a ~1 cm fit is his call.
- **The shotgun and pulse rifle support hands** are still the values he rejected in the headset.
  The kit's wrap branch reproduces them exactly, so re-running it cannot help; this needs to
  know *how* they were wrong.
- **Ballistics / CombatFX for the arc** — deferred by the owner. The arc currently borrows
  `AE_LoaderCageSpark`, which he described as "that ugly purple ball".
- **Multiplayer** — explicitly out of scope this pass. `muzzle.zs` is un-gated on his word and
  can desync a net game; the spine is local-fill for the same reason.
- **Tuning numbers.** The knife's slash thresholds, its shove, and the loader's arc damage and
  chain are invented defaults on sliders. Nobody has tuned them.

## 5. The traps — paid for, do not pay again

### My own measurements were wrong more often than the pack was

Every one of these was reported to the owner as a pack fault before being found out.

| I reported | It was |
|---|---|
| "no off arm on the loader" | I checked layer 2211, a dead constant. The arm is on 2200 via `hands.zs:694`. The constant is now deleted. |
| "the smartgun's ready beep never plays" | It plays. I grepped a log recorded with `rsr_debug` **off**. With it on: `rsr t278 sound SMARTUP`. |
| "the knife's grip is 2/8" | I scored a fit **I had invented** (Ermac's pistol hand, no rotation) instead of the shipped pose. Cut as shipped, the handle is inside the hand at three heights. |
| "the reload stalls before CARRY" | The harness reused one scratch config; `rsr_grab_with 1` from an earlier run leaked in and made the grab mask 0. |
| "dropped guns destroy themselves" | True but unreachable: nothing in the TC spawns the bare weapon actors. |
| enclosure 5/8, 6/8, 7/8 | The check measured the wrong thing **three times** — sliced at the fit's *shift*, then centred on the receiver, then cut perpendicular to F on a grip running along U. |

**The rule that falls out of this: open the sheet.** `000_READ_THIS_FIRST` §6 says it and it
was right every time — the cross-section was correct about the knife when my number was wrong.

### Engine and ZScript

- **`out Vector3` is a JIT abort**, not a compile error: `Unknown REGT value passed to
  EmitPARAM`. Write results to members. (Cost: the spine's first draft.)
- **ZScript identifiers are case-insensitive.** Five collisions here: `claw`/`Claw()`,
  `look`/`Look()`, `pull`/`Pull()`, `held`, `punch`/`Punch()`.
- **ZScript will not dereference `.z` off a parenthesised expression.** Put the vector on its
  own line.
- **`String.Format("%s", aSpriteID)` aborts the VM** and compiles clean.
- **A sprite frame exists only if a state names it.** A `FrameIndex` for a frame no state names
  is silently never drawn — that is why `AEAR ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTU` lists every letter.
- **The TC replaces every stock Doom monster with a spawner.** `Actor.Spawn('DoomImp')` gets
  `EnemySpawner4`. Use `XenomorphAT` with `NO_REPLACE`.
- **The test overlay's drivers must be in the SAME lump** the pack's `ZSCRIPT` includes — "a
  later ZSCRIPT lump cannot be seen from the pack's".
- **A harness that reuses a config lies.** The engine writes cvars back on quit.
  `run.py` copies `template.ini` fresh every run. Deleting the config instead is worse: with
  none at all there are no bindings and the console batch never runs.

### Melee, from `VR_MELEE_ON_QUESTZDOOM_2026-10-24.md`

That document is the reference for anything in this area. It found three defects in loader
melee that had already been proved working:

- **Speed alone is not a swing** — "raising the axe to strike was itself a hit". Needs
  direction **and** displacement.
- **A cut iterator with no reachability test** kills through walls. Trace from the **hand**, not
  the eyes.
- **Sounds on the player** arrive from inside your chest.
- **`DMG_THRUSTLESS` on every cut** leaves bodies standing exactly where they were.
