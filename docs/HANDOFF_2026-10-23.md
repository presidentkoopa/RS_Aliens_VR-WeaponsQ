## 2. Hand placement — read before touching a number

`zscript/rsq/handtable.zs` holds, per gun index: GunPose/SupportPose/Trigger (hand mesh frames,
NEVER changed), GunHook/GunRot, SupportHook/SupportRot (cm in the hand-model frame: x left,
y up, z back; the hook is added by `RSQ_Place.Layer` in `ModifyBobLayer3D`).

History that matters:
- The offline fits (`fits.json` -> table) were never drawn by the engine until 2026-10-22
  (the bug in §1). When they finally were, every long-gun hand sat a grip or two behind the
  grip. The offline transform chain is therefore suspect for this pack (its weapon blocks
  carry their own Offset / ZOffset / Scale, unlike BD22's). Do not trust `fits.json` for AE
  until the chain is checked against an engine render.
- The owner approved the hands he saw in the headset = hook 0. The table is therefore:
  shotgun, flamer = 0; pistols = (0, +1.5, +2.5); rifles = (0, +0.5, 0) support (0,-1.5,0);
  satchel GunRot y flipped 180 (owner: wrist 12 -> 6 o'clock, unverified by eye);
  SMARTGUN = REFITTED 2026-10-23 from the mesh with the kit's renderer (`tools/smartgun_fit.py`,
  sheet `docs/renders/smartgun_fit.png`, variants in `smartgun_fit_*_variants.png`): the gun
  hand is a fist (pose F, no trigger frame) on the rear bicycle handlebar, wrist up, fingers
  down round the brake lever, GunHook (-43.78 1.2 -0.96) GunRot (0 90 -90); the off hand is
  the handlebar fist (pose C) on the left handle past the drum, knuckles forward like a punch,
  BD22's minigun turn, SupportHook (-9.69 -16.75 2.52) SupportRot (10.21 16.06 95.16). Rotation
  order in the table = renderer's (yaw about y, pitch about x, roll about z). NOT yet confirmed
  by the owner in the headset; the support hand only shows while two-hand steadied.
- The owner tunes with the sliders in the headset and sends numbers; bake them into the
  table (fwd subtracts from z, up adds to y, side adds to x; support likewise; satchel
  yaw/pitch/roll add to GunRot), then REGENERATE `MODELDEF.aedual` (the off-hand copies have
  the hook baked into Offset = (0+x, -24+y, -10+z) because a DECORATE weapon has no
  ModifyBobLayer3D) — the generator is inline in the session log; a 20-line script
  (`tools/gen_aedual_modeldef.py`) is worth writing first thing.

## 3. The rig (render on the real engine, no headset)

`tools/rig/` holds the pieces that are not in the kit: `qz_linux_glue.cpp` (replaces the
Android/OpenXR layer; adds CCMD `qz_shot <file.ppm> [eye]`), `build.sh`, `runae.sh`,
`pad_csv.py`. Build per the kit's quest_test_rig instructions, then:

    runae.sh NAME "console script"    # env PACK=<pk3 in packs/>, EXTRA=<probe pk3>
    cp config/qzdoom.ini.base config/qzdoom.ini   # BEFORE EVERY RUN: the engine saves cvars

Console: `qz_hmd/qz_rhand/qz_lhand "x y z pitch yaw roll"` (m, deg; hand pitch 30 = level,
yaw 90 = gun points left = side view), `qz_rtrigger/qz_ltrigger/qz_rgrip/qz_lgrip 0|1`,
`qz_lbuttons 256` = X. `+attack` from the console does nothing in VR mode. Alt fire = grip
then trigger. `printinv` before/after is the ammo check. `summon SynthAT` + `notarget` gives
a target (never `freeze`: it stops projectiles). Shots need >= 4 tics apart.
`tools/probe/` = the probe event handlers (psprite layers, reload phase, ghosts, monsters).
`tools/magtest.py` scripts a magazine reload from the rsr_guns table.
Off-hand local frame for reload geometry (rs.offLocal): x forward, y left, z up, cm;
world = rhand + (-y, z, -x)/100 with the gun pointing forward.
Shotgun pump grab: 41 cm ahead of the gun controller, pull 12.6 cm; port 12.2 ahead, 1.6
below, push up then forward. Rifle launcher pump: 42.5 ahead, 8.5 below.

## 5. Engine facts you will need (all read from the 9afc750 source)

- `hw_weapon.cpp:332`: a layer whose caller != ReadyWeapon is placed via the OFF-HAND
  weapon's psprite; `:941-961` call `ModifyBobLayer3D` only when that psprite exists and the
  layer has PSPF_ADDBOB. The off-hand weapon must be brought up through `PendingWeapon`.
- `models.cpp:221`: a layer draws on the off controller only when its caller is
  player->OffhandWeapon. Hence RSQ_GunSide (inventory) for main-hand layers, RSQ_Hands
  (weapon) for off-hand layers, and the off-hand gun itself for its own hand (MODELDEF.aedual).
- MODELDEF binds to the exact class; subclasses get nothing. The parser keeps every slot's
  frame across FrameIndex lines of a block (so N values = N blocks, not N slots).
- PSP_OFFHANDWEAPON = 1000000. Weapon states only on weapon callers (hand states for the
  off gun live in RSQ_Hands).
- A model drawn in a block with `Scale -1.1 ...` is mirrored: a quad on it needs mirrored u.
- `vr_secondary_button_mappings`: main grip is a shift key; grip+trigger = +altattack.
